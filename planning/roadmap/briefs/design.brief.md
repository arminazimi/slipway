## Goal
Write the brief every design and layout task follows: principles, the five references and what to take from each, type and color direction, density, and what to avoid.

## Files

**Create**
- `docs/design/brief.md`

Touch no other file. If one must change, stop and say why in the issue.

## Steps
1. References: Next.js (structure: sidebar, on-this-page, pager), Tailwind (type and spacing), Stripe (code blocks and language switch), Supabase (home cards and section tabs), Starlight (right-to-left and i18n).
2. For each, two screenshots described in words (no copied assets) and the exact patterns to reuse.
3. Direction: neutral grays, one accent, sans body, generous whitespace, dense sidebar.
4. Fonts to evaluate (open licenses only) and the pick with reasons.
5. Anti-goals: serif body, beige backgrounds, decorative display type.

## Done when
- [ ] The brief names the font choice, the accent color family, and the layout widths.
- [ ] The maintainer approved it in the issue.
- [ ] `npm run check` passes.
