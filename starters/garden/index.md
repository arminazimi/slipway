This is a digital garden: a set of notes that change over time instead of a feed of finished posts. A note starts as a seedling, gains shape, and becomes evergreen when it is something worth standing behind.

The garden index reads the `stage` and `tended` front matter of every note, and each note lists the notes that link to it. To add one, copy `notes/why-a-garden.md`, change its `title`, `note`, and `stage`, and link to it from another note.
