A blog is a timeline: the newest post is the most visible, and an older post that is still right looks abandoned. A garden is the opposite. Notes stay where they are, change as I learn, and link to each other, so the useful thing is the shape of the whole, not the order it was written in.

The permission to publish something unfinished is the point. A seedling is a note I would not turn into a post, but it is still worth writing down, because the next note can disagree with it. See [how notes grow](stages-of-growth.html).

Nothing here is deleted. A note that turns out wrong is marked, not removed, so the links that point at it keep their meaning.
