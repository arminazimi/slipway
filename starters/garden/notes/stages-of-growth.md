Every note carries a `stage` in its front matter, and the garden index groups notes by it. The three words are a promise about how much to trust the note, not about how long it is.

- **Seedling** — an idea I am still working out. It may contradict yesterday's note, and it may disappear.
- **Budding** — the shape is there, the examples are not. I would quote it with a caveat.
- **Evergreen** — I would stand behind it. It still changes, but slowly, and [tending](tending-over-time.html) is mostly pruning.

The stage is a claim I make to myself as much as to a reader, which is why it is part of the file and not a label in the text: moving a note forward should feel like an edit worth recording in `tended`. This note is budding because the definitions are settled but the examples are thin, and [the note on why a garden](why-a-garden.html) is the one that set the terms.
