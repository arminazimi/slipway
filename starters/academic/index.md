I am an assistant professor of Computational Linguistics at the University of the North. I study how writing systems shape the software we build: how text is stored, segmented, rendered, and read across languages, with a focus on Persian and other right-to-left scripts.

My current projects look at static publishing for low-resource languages, evaluation of multilingual language models, and the typography of mixed-direction text. I teach courses on computational linguistics and multilingual web publishing.
