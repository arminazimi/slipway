## Introduction to Computational Linguistics

*Semester:* Fall 2026 · *Level:* Undergraduate

Text processing from the ground up: tokenization, morphology, and evaluation. The course uses Persian, English, and Arabic corpora side by side, so students see how the same method behaves in different scripts.

## Multilingual Web Publishing

*Semester:* Spring 2026 · *Level:* Graduate

Static sites, translation workflows, and right-to-left layout. Students publish a small bilingual site and write about the decisions they made: which text is shared, what gets translated, and how the two versions stay in step.

## Writing Systems and Software

*Semester:* Fall 2025 · *Level:* Graduate

How scripts reach the screen: encodings, shaping, bidirectional text, and fonts. The reading is half linguistics and half documentation from browsers, operating systems, and typesetting engines.
