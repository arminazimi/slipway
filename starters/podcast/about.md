Slipway Podcast is a small show about making things for the web: pages that load fast, text
that stays readable, and tools that do one thing well. It is recorded twice a month by
[Ada Rahimi](/authors/ada/).

## Subscribe

The feed is an ordinary RSS file with the iTunes namespace, so every podcast app can read it.

- [The feed](/feed.xml) (RSS)
- [Every episode](/episodes/)
- [The transcripts](/transcripts/episode-1.html)

Put the address of your published site in `url` in `_config.yml` before you submit the feed to a
directory: the entries need absolute URLs.

## The episodes

Each episode is a page under `_posts/` with its show notes, a link to the audio file, and a link
to its transcript. The audio files live in `audio/`.
