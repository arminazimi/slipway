## The problem

Checkout in the client's grocery app took four screens. Two thirds of the people who reached the basket left before paying; the support inbox was full of questions about delivery slots.

## What we did

We watched twelve people order groceries in a lab, then rebuilt the flow around the one decision that matters at that point: when the order arrives. Address, slot, payment, and confirmation now live on one screen, in that order, with the total always visible.

## What shipped

- One checkout screen with three collapsible sections.
- Delivery slots fetched once and shown with the price difference.
- The confirmation screen offers the receipt as a link, not a download.

## The result

Drop-off fell from 66% to 31% in the first month after release. The support inbox saw 40% fewer questions about slots.
