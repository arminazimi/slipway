## The problem

The scheduling product had a backlog ranked by the loudest customers. The team wanted evidence, but a six-week research project would have missed the quarter.

## What we did

Five days: two days of interviews with eight clinics, one day of synthesis, one day of sketches, one day of tests with five schedulers. The clinic staff were recruited through the client's own support list, and each session ended with a walk through a paper prototype of the next scheduling flow.

## What shipped

- A ranking of the backlog by how often a problem came up in the sessions.
- Three flows tested on paper; one went to design with the main objection already answered.

## The result

The team shipped the rescheduling flow instead of the reporting dashboard it had planned. Support tickets about rescheduling dropped by a third in the month after release.
