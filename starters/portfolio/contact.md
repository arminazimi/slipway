## Tell us about the project

Send three things: what the product does, what is not working, and when you need it. A link to the product is more useful than a deck.

Email: [hello@studionorth.example](mailto:hello@studionorth.example)

## What happens next

We answer within two working days. If the project fits, we book a call of thirty minutes and send a scope for the first three weeks within a week of that call.

## What we do not do

- Ongoing maintenance retainers.
- Projects that start in less than a week.
- Work where the last word on design belongs to someone outside the team.
