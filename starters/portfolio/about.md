## How we work

We start with a week of interviews and a review of what the team already knows. The rest of the project alternates between design and testing: a week of work ends with a session with real users, and the next week starts from what it showed.

## What we charge

A fixed weekly rate, agreed before the project starts, with a written scope for the first three weeks. There are no surprise invoices.

## Who we are

Two product designers and one researcher. We work in English and Persian, in person in Berlin or remotely.
